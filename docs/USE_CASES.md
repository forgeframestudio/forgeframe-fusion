# Initial Use Cases

These scenarios define what ForgeFrame Fusion should eventually handle through the same orchestration core.

## Creative production
Turn reference material plus a creative goal into finished artwork while preserving specified identity, branding, composition, or product constraints.

## Software and game development
Break a product goal into architecture, implementation, asset, testing, debugging, and documentation tasks and route each to appropriate capabilities.

## Business building
Coordinate research, branding, ecommerce, content, operations, analytics, and implementation while preserving one project context.

## Complex life administration
Help a person organize difficult multi-step administrative problems by gathering facts, identifying relevant resources, producing documents, tracking dependencies, and surfacing next actions. High-stakes domains retain human review and authoritative-source verification.

These are test domains, not promises that one model can perform every task. Fusion's job is to coordinate available, authorized capabilities and clearly expose what could or could not be completed.
