# ForgeFrame Fusion Principles

## Human outcome first
Fusion starts with what the person is trying to accomplish, not which AI product they should learn.

## Capability, not brand
Providers are replaceable. Capabilities are the stable abstraction.

## Preserve context
The user should not have to repeatedly explain constraints, preferences, successful work, or why a previous result failed.

## Repair, don't restart
When one stage fails, preserve successful work and repair the smallest responsible stage.

## Parallel when safe
Independent work should execute concurrently to reduce unnecessary waiting.

## Access matters
Cost and availability are routing constraints. A useful system should be able to prefer free, local, or already-authorized capabilities when requested.

## Privacy by design
Personal and sensitive information must not be sprayed across providers. Workflows classify data and minimize what each specialist receives.

## Human agency
Fusion assists decisions; it does not pretend uncertainty is certainty or silently make consequential choices for the user.

## Verifiable improvement
Claims that Fusion is faster, cheaper, or higher quality must be demonstrated with benchmarks, not marketing language.
